# The report email

What lands in the inbox and why each section is there. The pipeline that produces it is in [how-it-works.md](how-it-works.md).

## Email anatomy

The subject and the header carry the cycle start in local time (`display_timezone`, `Europe/Warsaw` by default, e.g. `pt 16.10 07:30`), not UTC; the push digest header and the shock-alert push use the same format. The provenance chips measure the age of cached fundamentals (`Dane z cache` past the 144 h TTL) from that same cycle start, not from the moment each symbol finished, so one email judges every row against one clock and the same inputs always render the same email.

The inbox preview comes from a hidden preheader, the first element of the HTML: the detail of the top lead item (HTML-escaped, since it comes from the council and the news), or `Brak sygnałów ponad próg · N zamkniętych predykcji` when the cycle has no lead. Invisible filler (`&zwnj;&nbsp;`, about 1.4 KB) follows it, so the mail client does not pull the header (`Czas rozpoczęcia…`) into the preview. The plain-text email has no preheader.

The lead, and therefore the subject, is in Polish end to end: a strong council consensus reads `NVDA +4,2%, rada zgodna: KUP` (KUP / SPRZEDAJ / TRZYMAJ from the shared dictionary in `src/domain/polish_labels.py`), a closed prediction reads `trafiona prognoza wzrostu` / `chybiona prognoza spadku`, and a CRITICAL quota alert carries the Polish text the adapter wrote. The English values (`BULLISH`, `BUY`) stay in the logs and the database.

Numbers in the lead, and therefore in the subject and the push subject, and the move in the shock-alert push (`📉 Szok rynkowy: NVDA -5,2%`) are written the Polish way (`src/domain/number_format.py`): a decimal comma (`NVDA -4,2%`, `NVDA (24% portfela)`), a no-break space (U+00A0) between thousands, an ASCII minus `-` so an inbox search for `-4,2%` finds the email, and half-up rounding (`4,25` → `4,3`); a move that rounds to zero reads `+0,0%`, never `-0,0%`. The email sections use the same formatter through `pct()`, `number()`, `number_exact()` and `money()` in `src/application/report_formatting.py`: prices and amounts (`$3 200,00`, `+810,04 zł`, with a no-break space before `zł`; an amount that rounds to zero has no minus), percentages, sentiment, RMSE, P/E, the NBP rates with the precision of the source (`4,2710`), the cycle duration and cost, and the Sunday recap. The public scorecard page keeps the decimal point: it renders the shared evidence labels with `DOT_NUMBERS`, and the significance verdict of the hit rate takes a `DecimalMark` (`src/domain/number_format.py`), so the email reads `p=0,61` while the page still reads `p=0.61`. Logs and stored values are unchanged.

The email is a **17 kB+ structured digest in Polish** (HTML + plain-text fallback), laid out in two zones (MAIL-17). Gmail clips a message above ~102 KB and hides its bottom first, so the part the reader acts on comes first and the context sits behind a separator. The plain text follows the same order and carries the same separator (`ZAPLECZE`); the sections marked HTML only are not in it.

**Na dziś** (today) — the quota banner and the lead open the email, then:

- 🕒 **NYSE session** — open / premarket / after-hours / weekend (`zoneinfo` America/New_York)
- 📊 **First screen: three numbers for the reader** — "Twój portfel od ostatniego maila" (the portfolio's change since the previous cycle: percent, amount in USD and, with the NBP rate, in PLN; see 💼 below), "Sygnały ponad próg" (saved predictions with a direction, SIDEWAYS left out) and "Rozliczone" (`3/5` hits among the settled predictions of the window, the same list as the closed-predictions section; `—` with "brak rozliczeń" for an empty window and "rozliczenia niedostępne" when the read failed, never `0/0`). The portfolio box is shown only to the owner with positions; any other recipient, and a cycle whose change cannot be measured, gets the other two boxes and no placeholder. The plain text opens with the same lines (`Sygnały ponad próg: 3`, `Rozliczone: 2/3 trafionych (ostatnie 24h)`). The pipeline counters (symbols, predictions, ignored, errors, duration) moved to the technical footer
- 📏 **Credibility bar** — one line under the first-screen numbers: the directional hit rate and its n from the same statistics as the accuracy history (`Wiarygodność modelu: trafność kierunkowa 57,5% (n=40, ostatnie 30 dni)`), pointing to the Track Record in the back office; `—` with "brak prognoz kierunkowych" when the window has no directional call, and no line when the statistics carry no directional counts
- 💼 **Your portfolio** (`portfolio_positions_enabled`) — real weights, unrealised P/L per position and in total (optionally in PLN), a STALE badge for an outdated positions table, the correlation-cluster warning, and the positions the cycle could not price. Under the totals, the change since the previous email (`Portfolio.change_since`, `src/domain/portfolio.py`): the amount and percent (`Zmiana od ostatniego maila: -$30,62 (-0,8%) · -112,73 zł`), the top 3 contributors by absolute change (`najwięcej: AMD -$87,05, NVDA +$56,43`; flat positions are left out) and the positions without a reference price (`bez punktu odniesienia: XDWD.DE`). Each move is weighted by the position's market value, so NVDA +10% at 75% of the capital is +7,5% of the portfolio. The previous price is rebuilt from the cycle's own delta (`current / (1 + Δ)`), for symbols below the volatility gate too, so the change costs no extra call; a cold-start symbol, a held position outside the watchlist and an unpriced one have no reference and stay out of both sides instead of counting as unchanged. After a pause (a reference older than 96 h, as in the volatility gate) the label names the reference date instead: `Zmiana od 19.09.2026`. The same number opens the email as the first summary box and, in the plain text, a `Twój portfel od ostatniego maila: -0,8% (-$30,62 · -112,73 zł)` line under the header; it is not in the subject. It appears in the HTML **and** the plain-text email. The legacy push, which sends the full plain text to Telegram/Slack when `messenger_digest_enabled = false`, now goes out separately from the email and leaves this section out, so P/L amounts never reach a messenger. In the per-subscriber fan-out (`subscriptions_enabled`), only the subscriber whose address matches `DIGEST_TO_EMAIL` (ignoring case and surrounding spaces) receives it; every other subscriber's email is built without the positions, clusters and held-position quotes, in the HTML and the plain text, so its lead and preheader have no "your position" tiers either; its subject is built from that portfolio-free lead too, while the owner's subject keeps them. Without `DIGEST_TO_EMAIL` nobody matches. Each withheld section is logged with a masked address (`f***@example.com`). The private web digest keeps the section
- 📊 **Closed predictions (24h)** — full **post-mortem** per resolved prediction (stocks **and** crypto): the original thesis (*why up/down* — `reasoning_text`), the actual move (`$A → $B (+C%)`), the ✅ Trafiona / ❌ Błędna verdict, and **why** (the `reflect` diagnosis for misses, thesis-confirmed for hits; a miss whose diagnosis was skipped shows that the direction did not hold instead of the placeholder text). The summary line under the list gives the same two hit rates as the accuracy history, with the same labels: directional (SIDEWAYS calls left out) next to total (SIDEWAYS included), each with its n. All from data already in `prediction_logs` — no extra LLM calls. The 24-hour window counts predictions *made* in the last 24 hours, so a Friday prediction settled on Monday never appears. With `settled_at_windows_enabled` (migration `029`) the section lists the predictions *settled* since the current cycle started instead, headed "Zamknięte predykcje (rozliczone w tym cyklu)" (`ZAMKNIĘTE PREDYKCJE (rozliczone w tym cyklu)` in the plain text), so each settlement appears in the email of the cycle that made it; the preheader count and the push digest scorecard count the same predictions. An empty window no longer leaves a blank: the section keeps its heading and says `Brak rozliczeń w tym cyklu.` (with `settled_at_windows_enabled`) or `Brak rozliczeń predykcji z ostatnich 24h.` (without it), matching the window in the heading. A failed read of the settlements is not shown as an empty window: the section says `⚠️ Nie udało się odczytać rozliczeń — w tym mailu ich brakuje (szczegóły w logach przebiegu).`, the preheader says `rozliczenia niedostępne` instead of `0 zamkniętych predykcji`, and the push digest scorecard says `rozliczenia niedostępne (błąd odczytu)`.
- 🔄 **Consensus shifts** — council flips and sentiment jumps against the previous cycle (HTML only)
- 🔄 **New reference point** — symbols whose last saved price was older than 96 h (a paused schedule), one line per reference day: `AAPL, MSFT: referencja sprzed 19.09.2026 — nowy punkt odniesienia`, with a note that the cycle saved a new price without analysis. They are not listed under "Pominięte (poniżej progu zmienności)", and the Krypto section labels such a ticker the same way instead of "poniżej progu"
- 🎯 **Strongest signals** — top BUY/SELL, colour-coded (priority 1: actionable); each card shows its rank and strength factors (`#1 · ruch 3,1% × pewność 85%`) instead of a bare strength number; with `calibrated_confidence_enabled`, raw → calibrated confidence and the calibration bucket's sample size (`85% → 58% po kalibracji (n kubełka: 40)`); ranked by confidence alone, and labelled so, while the ML model is on cold start
- 🚨 **Warning signals** — divergence + AV/LLM conflict + low signal
- 🔮 **Predictions table** — price · Δ (per cycle) · trend · forecast (next cycle) `+X,YZ%` (`— (ML: cold-start)` while XGBoost has no trained weights, instead of presenting the no-change baseline as a `+0,00%` forecast) · confidence · sentiment · news count · **model signal** (`Sygnał modelu`: KUP / SPRZEDAJ / TRZYMAJ, derived from the trend; the reasoning block and the plain text say `Sygnał modelu: KUP` too). The column used to be headed `Rekomendacja`, which contradicted the disclaimer; the Polish labels for the model signal and the council verdict live in one domain dictionary, and a sideways model signal and a council HOLD share one label, TRZYMAJ (the strongest-signals section keeps OBSERWUJ, which means no signal at all). The dictionary is `src/domain/polish_labels.py`, shared by the email, the lead and the subject. Each row is tagged with its **sector** next to the company name (stocks → Polish sector e.g. `Cyberbezpieczeństwo`, ETFs → `ETF`, crypto → `Krypto`); mapping in `report_formatting.SECTORS`. Company names come from `report_formatting.COMPANY_NAMES`; a guard test fails when any symbol in `config.toml` (`symbols`, `symbols_etf`, `crypto_symbols`, `risk_symbols`) has no name, so adding a ticker means adding its name too — otherwise it would render as a bare ticker (`S`, `VB`) in the lead, subject, tables and the Risk Watch section.
- 📈 **Forecast chart** — inline div bars (model forecasts only; hidden on ML cold start)
- 💡 **Reasoning** + 📰 **Top news** (clickable `<a href>` links to the original articles); under the expected move, each prediction shows its 80% interval for the return until settlement, "80% przedział: -2,1% … +2,4% (n=57)" with `n` the size of the calibration window, or "80% przedział: za mało historii (12/20)" when the asset class has fewer than 20 settled predictions (also in the plain-text email)
- 🪙 **Krypto** — dedicated section listing every tracked crypto ticker (price · Δ · trend/forecast for `saved`, "poniżej progu" for `ignored`). Rendered from the domain asset class (`Asset.asset_type`, surfaced as `SymbolResult.asset_class`), **independent of the 5% volatility gate** — so crypto stays visible even on a quiet day instead of vanishing into the "Pominięte" chips.

**Zaplecze** (back office) — behind a separator headed `🗄️ Zaplecze` with the note `Kontekst i kontrola: …`:

- 🚨 **Risk Watch** — macro hedges, drawdowns from the 30-day peak and the NBP rates
- 🎯 **Portfolio Radar** — correlation clusters of the watchlist ("43 symbols = 3 bets"), plus VaR/CVaR (`portfolio_var_enabled`) and a stress test (`stress_test_enabled`), from free `price_snapshots` history paired by UTC day. A statistic without enough history is listed as "za mało historii (n/60)" (VaR) or "(n/20)" (correlations, betas) instead of being shown from a few points; the plain-text email carries the same block
- 🛰️ **Alpha Signals** — insiders, analysts, options, social, earnings and the FRED yield curve (HTML only)
- 📈 **Track Record** (`equity_curve_enabled`, `calibration_curve_enabled`, `lessons_enabled`) — paper-PnL equity curve built as an equal-weight portfolio, one point per cycle (UTC day), SIDEWAYS held as cash, with the assumptions and the 2026-10-18 methodology change printed under it; the directional hit rate with a Wilson interval on `n_eff`, naive "always BULLISH / BEARISH / SIDEWAYS" baselines on the same predictions and a binomial-test verdict; Brier score, Brier Skill Score, log loss and the Murphy decomposition above the reliability table (ECE secondary); lessons learned. LLM-fallback rows (`llm_analysis_failed`) are excluded from every number. With `interval_coverage_enabled` (migration `032`) it also compares the realised coverage of the stored 80% intervals with the nominal 80%, with the exchangeability caveat printed under it.
- 🎯 **Accuracy history** — two hit rates over the last 30 days, side by side and labelled by what they count, each with its 95% Wilson interval: the **directional** hit rate ("Trafność kierunkowa (bez prognoz „bez ruchu”)": BULLISH/BEARISH calls only, the definition the Track Record uses) and the **total** hit rate ("Trafność łączna (z prognozami „bez ruchu”)": SIDEWAYS calls included). The total is `get_accuracy_stats()['mean_accuracy']`, which position sizing reads; its meaning is unchanged, and the directional counts come from the same rows (`directional_count`, `directional_correct_count`). Sizing also reads `n_eff`, the effective sample of the same rows grouped by UTC day (`significance.effective_n`, as in the Track Record): below `MIN_HIT_SAMPLE = 20` independent bets (`src/domain/sizing.py`), or when `n_eff` is missing, the hit rate is treated as cold start, so a single lucky call can never unlock the "Pozycja pełna 4-5%" band
- 🩺 **Model scorecard**, 🏆 **council ranking** and 🗳️ **council vote history** (HTML only)
- 📊 **Portfolio mood** — average sentiment, most positive/negative, high-confidence count
- 📈 **Δ chart** — inline div bars, change since last cycle
- 🧠 **Self-Reflection** — lessons learned per symbol (purple box)
- 💡 **Worth a look (sectors in motion)** — peer suggestions computed **from the current cycle only, zero extra API calls**: when a stock sector runs hot (its strongest monitored move `|Δ| ≥ 3%` or mean `|sentiment| ≥ 0.3`), the report suggests notable peers in that sector you don't yet monitor (curated `report_formatting.PEERS`), e.g. *Cyberbezpieczeństwo gorące (CRWD +6,0%, PANW +4,2%) — rozważ: ZS, FTNT, CYBR*. Top 3 sectors, 3 peers each; section hidden when nothing is hot.
- 📈 **Sentiment vs price correlation** (scatter plot)
- 💸 **Cycle cost** — paid calls of the cycle; a free cycle says so (HTML only)
- ⏸ **Ignored** (chip list) + ⚠️ **Errors**

**Footer.** Under the disclaimer, a technical line carries the pipeline counters that used to fill the first screen: `Symboli: 7 · Predykcje: 3 · Pominięte: 3 · Błędy: 1 · Czas trwania: 74,2s` (errors in red when there are any; `Symboli: 7 · predykcji: 3 · pominiętych: 3 · błędów: 1 · czas trwania: 74,2s` in the plain text). The last line of the HTML and the plain-text email says which run and which code produced the mail: `przebieg #1234 · commit 6eff942 · logi`. `main_agent.main()` builds a `RunContext` (`src/application/run_context.py`) once per cycle from the GitHub Actions environment (`GITHUB_RUN_ID`, `GITHUB_SHA`, `GITHUB_EVENT_NAME`, `GITHUB_SERVER_URL`, `GITHUB_REPOSITORY`) and passes it as `ReportContext.run_context`. The "logi" link to the run's logs appears only inside GitHub Actions (`GITHUB_ACTIONS=true`); a local run shows `przebieg #local · commit local` and no link. The run logs of a public repository are public, so the footer carries nothing beyond the run number, the short SHA and that link; the web digest (`web_digest_enabled`) and the legacy push get the same line.

**Investment disclaimer.** Every report email states that it is generated automatically by a statistical model backed by an LLM, for information and education only, and is not an investment recommendation, investment, tax or legal advice, or an offer to buy or sell (`INVESTMENT_DISCLAIMER` in `src/application/report_formatting.py`). It sits in the fixed HTML footer, above the technical line and the run line, and closes the plain-text body just before them, so it renders unconditionally: in an empty cycle, in every per-subscriber email of the fan-out, in the web digest and in the legacy push. The Sunday recap email ends with the same paragraph. The push digest carries a one-line variant (`ℹ️ Sygnały modelu nie stanowią rekomendacji inwestycyjnej.`) as its last line; its length is reserved in the 1500-character budget before the hard clamp, so truncation never cuts it. The day-1 onboarding section and the shock-alert push do not repeat it.

**Size budget.** Gmail clips messages above ~102 KB and hides the rest, footer and disclaimer included. The HTML is compacted (whitespace between tags collapsed to one space, which renders identically), and a test keeps a typical 8-symbol cycle with a full 7-persona council under 95 KB. A 45-symbol big-move day is still far over budget until council tables and reasoning blocks collapse outside the top signals — tracked as an expected failure until the report snapshot test lands.

All sections are **conditional** — they only render when data exists. The first cold-start cycle produces a concise report; once you have 50+ cycles behind you, accuracy sparklines and history kick in.
