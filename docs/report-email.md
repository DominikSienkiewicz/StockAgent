# The report email

What lands in the inbox and why each section is there. The pipeline that produces it is in [how-it-works.md](how-it-works.md).

## Sections in render order

Every section of the cycle email, in the order `build_html_report` renders it (the registry `REPORT_SECTIONS` in [`src/application/reporting/registry.py`](../src/application/reporting/registry.py), one `src/application/report_<name>.py` module per section), with its registry key, its heading as the email prints it, the condition that shows it and its state in the committed [`config.toml`](../config.toml). Most sections hide themselves when they have no data. [`tests/test_docs_report_sections.py`](../tests/test_docs_report_sections.py) reads every `<h2>` / `<h3>` heading of `src/application/report_*.py` and fails when one is missing here (DOC-15); [`tests/test_docs_contract.py`](../tests/test_docs_contract.py) fails when the `Key` column stops matching `REPORT_SECTIONS` one to one and in order (MAIL-22). The details of each section follow under [Email anatomy](#email-anatomy).

A separate email goes out after the Sunday Slow Loop: `📅 Tydzień StockAgenta`, the weekly recap with its `🛡️ Wybronieni dysydenci rady` block, with `weekly_recap_enabled` (`true` in `config.toml`) and at least 5 closed predictions in the week; it costs one LLM call ([configuration](configuration.md)). The section needs no LLM call and no sample threshold, so a week below 5 closed predictions (or a paused week) gets it as its own email, `💸 Koszt tygodnia StockAgenta`. With `council_redundancy_enabled` it adds a `🧮 Rada: 7 person ≈ 2,1 niezależnego głosu` line (Cohen's kappa between the personas, N_eff, the most agreeing pair); the daily email then drops the `SILNY KONSENSUS` badge of the council section when N_eff < 1.5 (WIA-45). With `council_redundancy_enabled` it adds a `🧮 Rada: 7 person ≈ 2,1 niezależnego głosu` line (Cohen's kappa between the personas, N_eff, the most agreeing pair); the daily email then drops the `SILNY KONSENSUS` badge of the council section when N_eff < 1.5 (WIA-45).

**Quiet-day email (MAIL-35).** With `digest_quiet_policy = "compact_when_quiet"` (`config.toml`; `always` in code) a cycle with nothing beyond the state sends a short email instead of the table above: no lead item, no settlement in the email's window, no watch shock since the last email, no symbol past the volatility gate (analysed or cut by a budget), no failed symbol and not the onboarding cycle (`should_send` in [`src/domain/digest_policy.py`](../src/domain/digest_policy.py)). It keeps `preheader`, `masthead`, `quota_banner`, `source_health`, `session` and `footer` in their usual order, with one block in between, `🌙 Cichy dzień — nic ponad stan` (`quiet_day`, [`src/application/report_quiet_day.py`](../src/application/report_quiet_day.py), only in `COMPACT_REPORT_SECTIONS`): the largest move with its effective threshold, `Żaden z N symboli nie przekroczył progu — cykl bez płatnej analizy.`, the owner's portfolio change since the last email (or, without positions, the watchlist's equal-weight average move) and a line saying the full report returns with the first signal, settlement or shock. The footer stays whole, with the disclaimer, the data sources and their attribution links and the providers' terms notice, because the terms require them on every email that shows the data. Every email recipient gets the same form; the subject, the archived copy (MAIL-24 keeps the full email, linked from the short one) and the push digest are unchanged. The golden snapshot `quiet_day_compact` pins it.

## Email anatomy

The subject and the header carry the cycle start in local time (`display_timezone`, `Europe/Warsaw` by default, e.g. `pt 16.10 07:30`), not UTC; the push digest header, the shock-alert push and the time of each alert in the quota banner (MAIL-59) use the same format. The provenance chips measure the age of cached fundamentals (`Dane z cache` past the 144 h TTL) from that same cycle start, not from the moment each symbol finished, so one email judges every row against one clock and the same inputs always render the same email.

**Private archive and the browser link (MAIL-24).** With `digest_archive_enabled` (and `DB_WRITES_ENABLED=true`) the Fast Loop uploads the owner's email to the private Supabase Storage bucket `digests` before sending it, writes one row with the subject, the lead and the top signals to the `digests` table (migration `061`), and renders the email again with `Otwórz w przeglądarce` after the start time in the header (`Wersja w przeglądarce (link ważny 7 dni): <url>` in the plain text). The link is a signed URL valid for 7 days, so the full email survives Gmail's clipping without any public page; the archived copy itself has no link. The push digest carries the same link under its header (`🔗 Pełny raport (link ważny 7 dni): …`). Subscribers other than the owner and the legacy push never get it — it opens the owner's email, portfolio included. A failed upload sends the email without the link (`archive` channel `failed` in the delivery outcome); a failed index write keeps the link. This replaced the public web digest U3 (BEZ-27).

The inbox preview comes from a hidden preheader, the first element of the HTML body: the detail of the top lead item (HTML-escaped, since it comes from the council and the news), or `Brak sygnałów ponad próg · N zamkniętych predykcji` when the cycle has no lead. Invisible filler (`&zwnj;&nbsp;`, about 1.4 KB) follows it, so the mail client does not pull the header (`Czas rozpoczęcia…`) into the preview. The plain-text email has no preheader.

The lead, and therefore the subject, is in Polish end to end: a strong council consensus reads `NVDA +4,2%, rada zgodna: KUP` (KUP / SPRZEDAJ / TRZYMAJ from the shared dictionary in `src/domain/polish_labels.py`), a closed prediction reads `trafiona prognoza wzrostu` / `chybiona prognoza spadku`, a held company reporting soon reads `MSFT (24% portfela): wyniki jutro po sesji` (DAT-20, `finnhub_earnings_calendar_enabled` with a portfolio position of at least 10%, today or tomorrow, right below a move of your position), and a CRITICAL quota alert carries the Polish text the adapter wrote. The English values (`BULLISH`, `BUY`) stay in the logs and the database.

Numbers in the lead, and therefore in the subject and the push subject, and the move in the shock-alert push (`📉 Szok rynkowy: NVDA -5,2%`) are written the Polish way (`src/domain/number_format.py`): a decimal comma (`NVDA -4,2%`, `NVDA (24% portfela)`), a no-break space (U+00A0) between thousands, an ASCII minus `-` so an inbox search for `-4,2%` finds the email, and half-up rounding (`4,25` → `4,3`); a move that rounds to zero reads `+0,0%`, never `-0,0%`. The email sections use the same formatter through `pct()`, `number()`, `number_exact()` and `money()` in `src/application/report_formatting.py`: prices and amounts (`$3 200,00`, `+810,04 zł`, with a no-break space before `zł`; an amount that rounds to zero has no minus), percentages, sentiment, RMSE, P/E, the NBP rates with the precision of the source (`4,2710`), the cycle duration and cost, and the Sunday recap. The public scorecard page keeps the decimal point: it renders the shared evidence labels with `DOT_NUMBERS`, and the significance verdict of the hit rate takes a `DecimalMark` (`src/domain/number_format.py`), so the email reads `p=0,61` while the page still reads `p=0.61`. Logs and stored values are unchanged.

The email is a **17 kB+ structured digest in Polish** (HTML + plain-text fallback), laid out in two zones (MAIL-17). The plain text follows the same order and carries every section that has a text renderer (MAIL-09); Alpha Signals, the Track Record, the council ranking and the inline charts stay HTML only. With `messenger_digest_enabled = false` the legacy push sends this text to Telegram and Slack, so a longer text reaches Telegram's 4096-character limit sooner. Gmail clips a message above ~102 KB and hides its bottom first, so the part the reader acts on comes first and the context sits behind a separator. The plain text follows the same order and carries the same separator (`ZAPLECZE`); the sections marked HTML only are not in it.

**Na dziś** (today) — the quota banner, the data-source status and the lead open the email, then:

- 📡 **Data-source status** (MAIL-21, `src/application/source_health.py`) — right under the quota banner, one line per provider: Finnhub, CoinGecko, Alpha Vantage, EDGAR, Reddit, FRED, NBP, Anthropic and OpenAI, each `OK`, `zdegradowane (3 symbole)` / `zdegradowane (4 błędne odpowiedzi)`, `niedostępne (otwarty obwód, 43 symbole pominięte)` or `wyłączone flagą`. The verdict reuses what the cycle already has: the process's HTTP circuits (`CircuitStatePort`, NIEZ-31) for "unavailable", the symbols' data-quality flags for "degraded" (`av_*` → Alpha Vantage, `llm_analysis_failed` / `llm_failover` → the main LLM provider), and the 429/5xx counts of the HTTP meter (NIEZ-24; for EDGAR and Reddit every 4xx, since they cut a client off with 403). `main_agent.enabled_sources` decides "disabled" from the same flags and keys the adapter factories use. The section appears only when a provider is degraded or unavailable; a provider switched off on purpose is not a problem, so a quiet day has no section.
- 👋 **Day 1** — the first cycle of a deployment welcomes the reader instead of a wall of skipped symbols, and under the welcome a `📖 Jak czytać ten mail` block (MAIL-22) says in four sentences how to read the rest: the confidence next to a forecast is the model's declared strength, not a probability; KUP / SPRZEDAJ / TRZYMAJ is a model and council signal, not investment advice; the portfolio figures are paper-PnL; the Track Record in the back office says how often the model is right.

- 🕒 **NYSE session** — open / premarket / after-hours / weekend / holiday (`zoneinfo` America/New_York). The session calendar (`src/domain/market_calendar.py`, ALR-15) holds the official NYSE holidays and early closes for 2026–2028 (https://www.nyse.com/markets/hours-calendars, read 2026-10-19): a holiday is named (`📅 Zamknięta (święto: Boże Narodzenie)`), an early-close day says so with its 1:00 p.m. ET close (`🟢 Otwarta (skrócona sesja do 13:00 ET)`), and the next open skips weekends and holidays (`Najbliższe otwarcie: pt 27.11 09:30 ET`). The open and close hours also come in the reader's time zone (`display_timezone`, MAIL-29): `Otwarcie za 3h 0min (15:30 czasu PL)`, `Najbliższe otwarcie: pon 19.10 09:30 ET (15:30 czasu PL)`; the US and Poland change the clock on different days, so the Polish hour shifts by one for a few weeks a year (`14:30` between 25.10 and 1.11), and a time zone other than `Europe/Warsaw` is labelled `czasu lokalnego`. Outside the covered years only weekends are known; a guard test fails when the calendar lacks the current year, so the list needs one more year every year
- 📊 **First screen: three numbers for the reader** (three cells of a layout table, `width: 33%` each, inline styles only — MAIL-23 replaced `display: flex`, which Outlook ignores; the 🎯 signal cards are the same kind of three-cell table) — "Twój portfel od ostatniego maila" (the portfolio's change since the previous cycle: percent, amount in USD and, with the NBP rate, in PLN; see 💼 below), "Sygnały ponad próg" (saved predictions with a direction, SIDEWAYS left out) and "Rozliczone" (`3/5` hits among the settled predictions of the window, the same list as the closed-predictions section; `—` with "brak rozliczeń" for an empty window and "rozliczenia niedostępne" when the read failed, never `0/0`). The portfolio box is shown only to the owner with positions; any other recipient, and a cycle whose change cannot be measured, gets the other two boxes and no placeholder. The plain text opens with the same lines (`Sygnały ponad próg: 3`, `Rozliczone: 2/3 trafionych (ostatnie 24h)`). The pipeline counters (symbols, predictions, ignored, errors, duration) moved to the technical footer
- 📏 **Credibility bar** — one line under the first-screen numbers: the directional hit rate and its n from the same statistics as the accuracy history (`Wiarygodność modelu: trafność kierunkowa 57,5% (n=40, ostatnie 30 dni)`), pointing to the Track Record in the back office; `—` with "brak prognoz kierunkowych" when the window has no directional call, and no line when the statistics carry no directional counts
- ⚡ **Between mails** (ALR-06, `shock_alerts_enabled`) — the shocks the hourly watch pushed since the last delivered mail: the start of the last fast-loop run with `report_delivered` in `cycle_runs` (migration `041`), or the last 24 h without one. One row per alert: symbol, move, direction, local time and the cycle's current price (`NVDA ▼ -6,20% (spadek) · czw 15.10 00:07 · teraz $131,20`). The price at alert time is not stored, so there is no "since the alert" move. A shock on a position of at least 10% of the portfolio is also a lead candidate in the position tier (`⚡ NVDA (24% portfela) -6,2% między mailami`), even when the cycle's own move is below the gate. With the watch paused the section is always empty and hides itself.
- 💼 **Your portfolio** (`portfolio_positions_enabled`) — real weights, unrealised P/L per position and in total in the position's currency, with `P/L zł` (cost at the NBP rate of the purchase date, value at the current rate) and `w tym kurs` (the currency part of it) when NBP rates are available, or an explicit `kurs z dnia zakupu nieznany` when the purchase date is unknown ([configuration](configuration.md)), a STALE badge for an outdated positions table, the concentration warning when one stock sector (`sectors` in `config.toml`, at least two positions) holds 40% of the capital or more, and the positions the cycle could not price. Under the totals, the change since the previous email (`Portfolio.change_since`, `src/domain/portfolio.py`): the amount and percent (`Zmiana od ostatniego maila: -112,73 zł (-0,8%)`), the top 3 contributors by absolute change (`najwięcej: AMD -320,51 zł, NVDA +207,78 zł`; flat positions are left out) and the positions without a reference price (`bez punktu odniesienia: XDWD.DE`). Each move is weighted by the position's market value, so NVDA +10% at 75% of the capital is +7,5% of the portfolio. With NBP rates the change is in złoty: both prices of a position are converted at its currency's current rate, so the line shows the instruments' move in PLN, not the currency move between two emails; a position without a rate is left out of both sides and named in the section's `Bez kursu NBP` note. Without any rate (NBP off) the amounts stay in the quote currency, as before. The previous price is rebuilt from the cycle's own delta (`current / (1 + Δ)`), for symbols below the volatility gate too, so the change costs no extra call; a cold-start symbol, a held position outside the watchlist and an unpriced one have no reference and stay out of both sides instead of counting as unchanged. After a pause (a reference older than 96 h, as in the volatility gate) the label names the reference date instead: `Zmiana od 19.09.2026`. The same number opens the email as the first summary box and, in the plain text, a `Twój portfel od ostatniego maila: -0,8% (-112,73 zł)` line under the header; it is not in the subject. It appears in the HTML **and** the plain-text email. The legacy push, which sends the full plain text to Telegram/Slack when `messenger_digest_enabled = false`, now goes out separately from the email and leaves this section out, so P/L amounts never reach a messenger. In the per-subscriber fan-out (`subscriptions_enabled`), only the subscriber whose address matches `DIGEST_TO_EMAIL` (ignoring case and surrounding spaces) receives it; every other subscriber's email is built without the positions, clusters and held-position quotes, in the HTML and the plain text, so its lead and preheader have no "your position" tiers either; its subject is built from that portfolio-free lead too, while the owner's subject keeps them. Without `DIGEST_TO_EMAIL` nobody matches. Each withheld section is logged with a masked address (`f***@example.com`).
