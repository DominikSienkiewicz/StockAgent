# The report email

What lands in the inbox and why each section is there. The pipeline that produces it is in [how-it-works.md](how-it-works.md).

## Email anatomy

The subject and the header carry the cycle start in local time (`display_timezone`, `Europe/Warsaw` by default, e.g. `pt 16.10 07:30`), not UTC; the push digest header and the shock-alert push use the same format. The provenance chips measure the age of cached fundamentals (`Dane z cache` past the 144 h TTL) from that same cycle start, not from the moment each symbol finished, so one email judges every row against one clock and the same inputs always render the same email.

The inbox preview comes from a hidden preheader, the first element of the HTML body: the detail of the top lead item (HTML-escaped, since it comes from the council and the news), or `Brak sygnałów ponad próg · N zamkniętych predykcji` when the cycle has no lead. Invisible filler (`&zwnj;&nbsp;`, about 1.4 KB) follows it, so the mail client does not pull the header (`Czas rozpoczęcia…`) into the preview. The plain-text email has no preheader.

The lead, and therefore the subject, is in Polish end to end: a strong council consensus reads `NVDA +4,2%, rada zgodna: KUP` (KUP / SPRZEDAJ / TRZYMAJ from the shared dictionary in `src/domain/polish_labels.py`), a closed prediction reads `trafiona prognoza wzrostu` / `chybiona prognoza spadku`, and a CRITICAL quota alert carries the Polish text the adapter wrote. The English values (`BULLISH`, `BUY`) stay in the logs and the database.

Numbers in the lead, and therefore in the subject and the push subject, and the move in the shock-alert push (`📉 Szok rynkowy: NVDA -5,2%`) are written the Polish way (`src/domain/number_format.py`): a decimal comma (`NVDA -4,2%`, `NVDA (24% portfela)`), a no-break space (U+00A0) between thousands, an ASCII minus `-` so an inbox search for `-4,2%` finds the email, and half-up rounding (`4,25` → `4,3`); a move that rounds to zero reads `+0,0%`, never `-0,0%`. The email sections use the same formatter through `pct()`, `number()`, `number_exact()` and `money()` in `src/application/report_formatting.py`: prices and amounts (`$3 200,00`, `+810,04 zł`, with a no-break space before `zł`; an amount that rounds to zero has no minus), percentages, sentiment, RMSE, P/E, the NBP rates with the precision of the source (`4,2710`), the cycle duration and cost, and the Sunday recap. The public scorecard page keeps the decimal point: it renders the shared evidence labels with `DOT_NUMBERS`, and the significance verdict of the hit rate takes a `DecimalMark` (`src/domain/number_format.py`), so the email reads `p=0,61` while the page still reads `p=0.61`. Logs and stored values are unchanged.

The email is a **17 kB+ structured digest in Polish** (HTML + plain-text fallback), laid out in two zones (MAIL-17). Gmail clips a message above ~102 KB and hides its bottom first, so the part the reader acts on comes first and the context sits behind a separator. The plain text follows the same order and carries the same separator (`ZAPLECZE`); the sections marked HTML only are not in it.

**Na dziś** (today) — the quota banner and the lead open the email, then:

- 🕒 **NYSE session** — open / premarket / after-hours / weekend / holiday (`zoneinfo` America/New_York). The session calendar (`src/domain/market_calendar.py`, ALR-15) holds the official NYSE holidays and early closes for 2026–2028 (https://www.nyse.com/markets/hours-calendars, read 2026-10-19): a holiday is named (`📅 Zamknięta (święto: Boże Narodzenie)`), an early-close day says so with its 1:00 p.m. ET close (`🟢 Otwarta (skrócona sesja do 13:00 ET)`), and the next open skips weekends and holidays (`Najbliższe otwarcie: pt 27.11 09:30 ET`). Outside the covered years only weekends are known; a guard test fails when the calendar lacks the current year, so the list needs one more year every year
- 📊 **First screen: three numbers for the reader** — "Twój portfel od ostatniego maila" (the portfolio's change since the previous cycle: percent, amount in USD and, with the NBP rate, in PLN; see 💼 below), "Sygnały ponad próg" (saved predictions with a direction, SIDEWAYS left out) and "Rozliczone" (`3/5` hits among the settled predictions of the window, the same list as the closed-predictions section; `—` with "brak rozliczeń" for an empty window and "rozliczenia niedostępne" when the read failed, never `0/0`). The portfolio box is shown only to the owner with positions; any other recipient, and a cycle whose change cannot be measured, gets the other two boxes and no placeholder. The plain text opens with the same lines (`Sygnały ponad próg: 3`, `Rozliczone: 2/3 trafionych (ostatnie 24h)`). The pipeline counters (symbols, predictions, ignored, errors, duration) moved to the technical footer
- 📏 **Credibility bar** — one line under the first-screen numbers: the directional hit rate and its n from the same statistics as the accuracy history (`Wiarygodność modelu: trafność kierunkowa 57,5% (n=40, ostatnie 30 dni)`), pointing to the Track Record in the back office; `—` with "brak prognoz kierunkowych" when the window has no directional call, and no line when the statistics carry no directional counts
- 💼 **Your portfolio** (`portfolio_positions_enabled`) — real weights, unrealised P/L per position and in total in the position's currency, with `P/L zł` (cost at the NBP rate of the purchase date, value at the current rate) and `w tym kurs` (the currency part of it) when NBP rates are available, or an explicit `kurs z dnia zakupu nieznany` when the purchase date is unknown ([configuration](configuration.md)), a STALE badge for an outdated positions table, the concentration warning when one stock sector (`sectors` in `config.toml`, at least two positions) holds 40% of the capital or more, and the positions the cycle could not price. Under the totals, the change since the previous email (`Portfolio.change_since`, `src/domain/portfolio.py`): the amount and percent (`Zmiana od ostatniego maila: -112,73 zł (-0,8%)`), the top 3 contributors by absolute change (`najwięcej: AMD -320,51 zł, NVDA +207,78 zł`; flat positions are left out) and the positions without a reference price (`bez punktu odniesienia: XDWD.DE`). Each move is weighted by the position's market value, so NVDA +10% at 75% of the capital is +7,5% of the portfolio. With NBP rates the change is in złoty: both prices of a position are converted at its currency's current rate, so the line shows the instruments' move in PLN, not the currency move between two emails; a position without a rate is left out of both sides and named in the section's `Bez kursu NBP` note. Without any rate (NBP off) the amounts stay in the quote currency, as before. The previous price is rebuilt from the cycle's own delta (`current / (1 + Δ)`), for symbols below the volatility gate too, so the change costs no extra call; a cold-start symbol, a held position outside the watchlist and an unpriced one have no reference and stay out of both sides instead of counting as unchanged. After a pause (a reference older than 96 h, as in the volatility gate) the label names the reference date instead: `Zmiana od 19.09.2026`. The same number opens the email as the first summary box and, in the plain text, a `Twój portfel od ostatniego maila: -0,8% (-112,73 zł)` line under the header; it is not in the subject. It appears in the HTML **and** the plain-text email. The legacy push, which sends the full plain text to Telegram/Slack when `messenger_digest_enabled = false`, now goes out separately from the email and leaves this section out, so P/L amounts never reach a messenger. In the per-subscriber fan-out (`subscriptions_enabled`), only the subscriber whose address matches `DIGEST_TO_EMAIL` (ignoring case and surrounding spaces) receives it; every other subscriber's email is built without the positions, clusters and held-position quotes, in the HTML and the plain text, so its lead and preheader have no "your position" tiers either; its subject is built from that portfolio-free lead too, while the owner's subject keeps them. Without `DIGEST_TO_EMAIL` nobody matches. Each withheld section is logged with a masked address (`f***@example.com`). The private web digest keeps the section
- 📊 **Closed predictions (24h)** — full **post-mortem** per resolved prediction (stocks **and** crypto): the original thesis (*why up/down* — `reasoning_text`), the actual move (`$A → $B (+C%)`), the ✅ Trafiona / ❌ Błędna verdict, under a miss one line from its decision receipt (WIA-56: `🧾 Kwit: stała na analogach #3f2a1b9c (0,91), #77aa0e12 (0,87) przy progu 3,0%` — the closest RAG analogues by short id and similarity and the effective threshold; also in the plain text), and **why** (the `reflect` diagnosis for misses, thesis-confirmed for hits; a miss whose diagnosis was skipped shows that the direction did not hold instead of the placeholder text). The summary line under the list gives the same two hit rates as the accuracy history, with the same labels: directional (SIDEWAYS calls left out) next to total (SIDEWAYS included), each with its n. All from data already in `prediction_logs` — no extra LLM calls. The 24-hour window counts predictions *made* in the last 24 hours, so a Friday prediction settled on Monday never appears. With `settled_at_windows_enabled` (migration `029`) the section lists the predictions *settled* since the current cycle started instead, headed "Zamknięte predykcje (rozliczone w tym cyklu)" (`ZAMKNIĘTE PREDYKCJE (rozliczone w tym cyklu)` in the plain text), so each settlement appears in the email of the cycle that made it; the preheader count and the push digest scorecard count the same predictions. An empty window no longer leaves a blank: the section keeps its heading and says `Brak rozliczeń w tym cyklu.` (with `settled_at_windows_enabled`) or `Brak rozliczeń predykcji z ostatnich 24h.` (without it), matching the window in the heading. A failed read of the settlements is not shown as an empty window: the section says `⚠️ Nie udało się odczytać rozliczeń — w tym mailu ich brakuje (szczegóły w logach przebiegu).`, the preheader says `rozliczenia niedostępne` instead of `0 zamkniętych predykcji`, and the push digest scorecard says `rozliczenia niedostępne (błąd odczytu)`.
- 🔄 **Consensus shifts** — council flips and sentiment jumps against the previous cycle (HTML only)
- 🔄 **New reference point** — symbols whose last saved price was older than 96 h (a paused schedule), one line per reference day: `AAPL, MSFT: referencja sprzed 19.09.2026 — nowy punkt odniesienia`, with a note that the cycle saved a new price without analysis. They are not listed under "Pominięte (poniżej progu zmienności)", and the Krypto section labels such a ticker the same way instead of "poniżej progu"
- 🎯 **Strongest signals** — top BUY/SELL, colour-coded (priority 1: actionable); each card shows its rank and strength factors (`#1 · ruch 3,1% × pewność 85%`) instead of a bare strength number; with `calibrated_confidence_enabled`, raw → calibrated confidence and the calibration bucket's sample size (`85% → 58% po kalibracji (n kubełka: 40)`); ranked by confidence alone, and labelled so, while the ML model is on cold start. With `equity_curve_enabled`, a line under each card gives the directional hit rate on that symbol over the track-record window, `Trafność kierunkowa na NVDA: 4/7 (30 dni, 95% CI 25–84%)`, or `za mało historii (n=3 < 5, 30 dni)` below 5 directional calls (WIA-35: one free read of the settled predictions, SIDEWAYS calls left out as in the Track Record; also in the plain-text email)
- 🚨 **Warning signals** — divergence + AV/LLM conflict + low signal
- 🔮 **Predictions table** — under the heading, the forecast horizon from the same calendar (`Horyzont prognozy: następny cykl · najbliższe zamknięcie NYSE: czw 15.10 16:00 ET`, also in the plain text): the next cycle scores the forecast at its own price, so with a daily pre-open cycle the forecast covers that session; then price · Δ (per cycle) · trend · forecast (next cycle) `+X,YZ%` (`— (ML: cold-start)` while XGBoost has no trained weights, instead of presenting the no-change baseline as a `+0,00%` forecast) · confidence · sentiment · news count · **model signal** (`Sygnał modelu`: KUP / SPRZEDAJ / TRZYMAJ, derived from the trend; the reasoning block and the plain text say `Sygnał modelu: KUP` too). The column used to be headed `Rekomendacja`, which contradicted the disclaimer; the Polish labels for the model signal and the council verdict live in one domain dictionary, and a sideways model signal and a council HOLD share one label, TRZYMAJ (the strongest-signals section keeps OBSERWUJ, which means no signal at all). The dictionary is `src/domain/polish_labels.py`, shared by the email, the lead and the subject. Each row is tagged with its **sector** next to the company name (stocks → Polish sector e.g. `Cyberbezpieczeństwo`, ETFs → `ETF`, crypto → `Krypto`); the mapping comes from `sectors`, `symbols_etf` and `crypto_symbols` in `config.toml` (`report_formatting.build_sector_map`), and a guard test fails when a configured symbol has no sector. Company names come from `report_formatting.COMPANY_NAMES`; a guard test fails when any symbol in `config.toml` (`symbols`, `symbols_etf`, `crypto_symbols`, `risk_symbols`) has no name, so adding a ticker means adding its name too — otherwise it would render as a bare ticker (`S`, `VB`) in the lead, subject, tables and the Risk Watch section.
- 📈 **Forecast chart** — inline div bars (model forecasts only; hidden on ML cold start)
- 💡 **Reasoning** + 📰 **Top news** (clickable `<a href>` links to the original articles); under the expected move, each prediction shows its 80% interval for the return until settlement, "80% przedział: -2,1% … +2,4% (n=57)" with `n` the size of the calibration window, or "80% przedział: za mało historii (12/20)" when the asset class has fewer than 20 settled predictions (also in the plain-text email); under it, the same per-symbol hit-rate line as the strongest-signal card (WIA-35)
- 🪙 **Krypto** — dedicated section listing every tracked crypto ticker (price · Δ · trend/forecast for `saved`, "poniżej progu" for `ignored`). Rendered from the domain asset class (`Asset.asset_type`, surfaced as `SymbolResult.asset_class`), **independent of the 5% volatility gate** — so crypto stays visible even on a quiet day instead of vanishing into the "Pominięte" chips.

**Zaplecze** (back office) — behind a separator headed `🗄️ Zaplecze` with the note `Kontekst i kontrola: …`:

- 🚨 **Risk Watch** — macro hedges, drawdowns from the 30-day peak and the NBP rates
- 🎯 **Portfolio Radar** — correlation clusters of the watchlist ("43 symbols = 3 bets"), plus VaR/CVaR (`portfolio_var_enabled`) and a stress test (`stress_test_enabled`), from free `price_snapshots` history paired by UTC day. A statistic without enough history is listed as "za mało historii (n/60)" (VaR) or "(n/20)" (correlations, betas) instead of being shown from a few points; the plain-text email carries the same block
- 🛰️ **Alpha Signals** — insiders, analysts, options, social, earnings and the FRED yield curve (HTML only). When EDGAR listed Form 4 filings in the window but none of them parsed, the insider cell says `brak danych EDGAR` and the insider source is left out of the Alpha Fusion Score (DAT-03, `InsiderFlowSnapshot.has_evidence`), instead of passing for a quiet window with no insider trades; a window with no Form 4 at all still shows `—` and votes neutral. A market purchase (code `P`, outside a Rule 10b5-1 plan) of at least 100k USD by an officer gets its own line under the table, e.g. `🟢 NVDA — CEO kupił akcje za 1,2 mln USD (Form 4 →)`, linking the filing on sec.gov (DAT-21: the adapter reads the price per share, the owner's role and the `aff10b5One` plan box from the same Form 4 XML; a filing without the box counts as discretionary, a 10% owner alone is not an insider buy). The insider cell and the Alpha Fusion weight still use net shares, which are unchanged until an A/B test
- 📈 **Track Record** (`equity_curve_enabled`, `calibration_curve_enabled`, `lessons_enabled`) — paper-PnL equity curve built as an equal-weight portfolio, one point per cycle (UTC day), SIDEWAYS held as cash, net of trading costs and labelled "paper-PnL (koszt 10 bps)" (WIA-50: `paper_cost_bps`, `crypto_paper_cost_bps`, charged on turnover only — see [configuration](configuration.md)), with the assumptions and the 2026-10-18 methodology change printed under it; a "Na tle rynku" line puts the agent next to two buy&hold benchmarks with the same costs (WIA-21) — the basket (the same symbols, long-only, in the same windows) and IVV (long-only in the prediction windows) — as "agent +1,2% · koszyk +3,4% · IVV +2,1%", followed by the excess return over the basket in percentage points and the market-relative hit rate (whether the direction got the sign of the return over IVV right, so a BULLISH call on a stock that falls 1% while IVV falls 3% is a hit), and the sparkline gets a second, grey row for the basket on a shared scale; without IVV snapshots the line says "brak danych benchmarku" instead of a blank number (timestamp convention in [configuration](configuration.md)); the directional hit rate with a Wilson interval on `n_eff`, naive "always BULLISH / BEARISH / SIDEWAYS" baselines on the same predictions and a binomial-test verdict; Brier score, Brier Skill Score, log loss and the Murphy decomposition above the reliability table (ECE secondary), all on directional predictions, with SIDEWAYS calls in a line of their own under it (`n` and Brier, WIA-39); lessons learned. A note under the equity summary says the entry is the price at the moment of the prediction — before the session, the previous close — so the result includes the opening gap a reader of the morning email cannot buy (WIA-34). When the cycle flagged predictions too old for `reflect` to score, a line under the heading gives their count, `⏳ 3 predykcje wygasły bez oceny` (WIA-49), in the compact version too. LLM-fallback rows (`llm_analysis_failed`) are excluded from every number. With `interval_coverage_enabled` (migration `032`) it also compares the realised coverage of the stored 80% intervals with the nominal 80%, with the exchangeability caveat printed under it. A change detector (NIEZ-20) runs Page-Hinkley over the directional hit rate per cycle (UTC day, SIDEWAYS left out, chronological) with pre-registered parameters in [`src/domain/change_detection.py`](../src/domain/change_detection.py) — tolerance `delta = 0.05`, threshold `2.0`, no alarm before 10 cycles. When it fires, the section says "Detektor zmiany (Page-Hinkley): trafność spadła istotnie od <date>" with the hit rate before and since that date, where the date is the first cycle after the peak of the cumulative statistic, and the same sentence goes to the top banner as a `WARNING` (`Detektor trafności`); the alarm is recomputed every cycle and is not stored in `quota_alerts`. With a few directional predictions per cycle a false alarm comes about once in several hundred cycles, and a 20-point drop shows up after about 20 cycles — with sparse predictions, or right after the cron resumes from a pause, the detector lags by weeks.
- 🎯 **Accuracy history** — two hit rates over the last 30 days, side by side and labelled by what they count, each with its 95% Wilson interval: the **directional** hit rate ("Trafność kierunkowa (bez prognoz „bez ruchu”)": BULLISH/BEARISH calls only, the definition the Track Record uses) and the **total** hit rate ("Trafność łączna (z prognozami „bez ruchu”)": SIDEWAYS calls included). The total is `get_accuracy_stats()['mean_accuracy']`, which position sizing reads; its meaning is unchanged, and the directional counts come from the same rows (`directional_count`, `directional_correct_count`). Sizing also reads `n_eff`, the effective sample of the same rows grouped by UTC day (`significance.effective_n`, as in the Track Record): below `MIN_HIT_SAMPLE = 20` independent bets (`src/domain/sizing.py`), or when `n_eff` is missing, the hit rate is treated as cold start, so a single lucky call can never unlock the "Pozycja pełna 4-5%" band
- 🩺 **Model scorecard**, 🏆 **council ranking** and 🗳️ **council vote history** (HTML only). The council ranking (personas with at least 5 settled votes in 90 days, migration `018`) is ordered by the lower bound of each persona's 95% Wilson interval (WIA-30), so a perfect 5/5 no longer outranks a strong 40/50. When the Track Record has hit-rate evidence (`equity_curve_enabled`), each persona also gets a significance verdict (WIA-22): a one-sided binomial test against the best naive strategy of the track-record window (always BULLISH, BEARISH or SIDEWAYS, since a persona votes BUY, SELL or HOLD), run on `n_eff` = votes / the design effect (n / n_eff) of that window's directional predictions, with Benjamini–Hochberg FDR (q = 0.10) across the personas, e.g. "nieodróżnialne od rzutu monetą (p=0,03, n=22, n_eff≈11)"; the interval and the ranking then use the same `n_eff`. The section says in one sentence that this is an approximation: the persona RPC returns only a hit rate and a vote count, so the baseline and `n_eff` come from the window's predictions, not from the votes. No migration. The model scorecard ends with a data-coverage line for the `track_record_days` window (DAT-16, `src/domain/coverage.py`): the share of expected sessions with a price snapshot (NYSE trading days for a watchlist with stocks — weekends and known NYSE holidays are not gaps — every UTC day for a crypto-only one; a morning snapshot counts for the session it closed), the longest gap with its dates, and the symbols with no settled prediction in the window, e.g. `Pokrycie danych: 87% sesji ze snapshotem ceny (26 z 30); najdłuższa luka 2026-08-17 – 2026-09-11 (20 sesji); bez rozliczeń w oknie: SSNLF.` The public scorecard page shows the same line with only the number of such symbols, never their names
- 📊 **Portfolio mood** — average sentiment, most positive/negative, high-confidence count
- 📈 **Δ chart** — inline div bars, change since last cycle
- 🧠 **Self-Reflection** — lessons learned per symbol (purple box)
- 💡 **Worth a look (sectors in motion)** — peer suggestions computed **from the current cycle only, zero extra API calls**: when a stock sector runs hot (its strongest monitored move `|Δ| ≥ 3%` or mean `|sentiment| ≥ 0.3`), the report suggests notable peers in that sector you don't yet monitor (curated `sector_peers` in `config.toml`, keyed by the `sectors` names), e.g. *Cyberbezpieczeństwo gorące (CRWD +6,0%, PANW +4,2%) — rozważ: ZS, FTNT, CYBR*. Top 3 sectors, 3 peers each; section hidden when nothing is hot.
- 📈 **Sentiment vs price correlation** (scatter plot)
- 💸 **Cycle cost** — paid calls of the cycle; a free cycle says so (HTML only). A source whose requests reported priced tokens shows the cost from tokens, the others the unit estimate marked "(szacunek)", with the price date (FIN-13, [configuration](configuration.md))
- 💸 **Skipped — cycle budget exhausted** (`Pominięte — wyczerpany budżet cyklu`, FIN-12) — symbols whose move passed the volatility gate after the cycle had spent `max_paid_llm_calls_per_cycle`, as chips with their move and a note that the price reference stays unchanged. They are not listed under "Ignored", and the crypto section calls such a ticker `budżet cyklu wyczerpany` instead of "poniżej progu".
- ⏸ **Ignored** (chip list) + ⚠️ **Errors**

**Footer.** Under the disclaimer, a technical line carries the pipeline counters that used to fill the first screen: `Symboli: 7 · Predykcje: 3 · Pominięte: 3 · Błędy: 1 · Czas trwania: 74,2s` (errors in red when there are any; `Symboli: 7 · predykcji: 3 · pominiętych: 3 · błędów: 1 · czas trwania: 74,2s` in the plain text). The last line of the HTML and the plain-text email says which run and which code produced the mail: `przebieg #1234 · commit 6eff942 · logi`. `main_agent.main()` builds a `RunContext` (`src/application/run_context.py`) once per cycle from the GitHub Actions environment (`GITHUB_RUN_ID`, `GITHUB_SHA`, `GITHUB_EVENT_NAME`, `GITHUB_SERVER_URL`, `GITHUB_REPOSITORY`) and passes it as `ReportContext.run_context`. The "logi" link to the run's logs appears only inside GitHub Actions (`GITHUB_ACTIONS=true`); a local run shows `przebieg #local · commit local` and no link. The run logs of a public repository are public, so the footer carries nothing beyond the run number, the short SHA and that link; the web digest (`web_digest_enabled`) and the legacy push get the same line.

**Investment disclaimer.** Every report email states that it is generated automatically by a statistical model backed by an LLM, for information and education only, and is not an investment recommendation, investment, tax or legal advice, or an offer to buy or sell (`INVESTMENT_DISCLAIMER` in `src/application/report_formatting.py`). It sits in the fixed HTML footer, above the technical line and the run line, and closes the plain-text body just before them, so it renders unconditionally: in an empty cycle, in every per-subscriber email of the fan-out, in the web digest and in the legacy push. The Sunday recap email ends with the same paragraph. The push digest carries a one-line variant (`ℹ️ Sygnały modelu nie stanowią rekomendacji inwestycyjnej.`) as its last line; its length is reserved in the 1500-character budget before the hard clamp, so truncation never cuts it. The day-1 onboarding section and the shock-alert push do not repeat it.

**Accessibility.** The HTML is a complete document, `<!DOCTYPE html><html lang="pl"><head><meta charset="utf-8">…</head><body>…</body></html>`, so a screen reader reads the Polish text with Polish phonetics; the stylesheet sits in the `<head>` and the preheader stays the first element of the body. Tables that only lay out content (the quota banner, the bar charts, the Track Record sparkline) carry `role="presentation"`, and every header cell of a data table is `<th scope="col">`. A coloured direction is never conveyed by colour alone: moves and forecasts read `▲ +0,42%` / `▼ -0,42%` (`direction_pct` in `src/application/report_formatting.py`, through the Polish formatter with an ASCII minus; a move that rounds to zero gets no arrow), including the "Pominięte" chips and the move column of the predictions table, which used to show an unsigned percentage, and a ✅ / ❌ always has "Trafiona" / "Błędna" (or "trafił" / "chybił") next to it. `contrast_ratio(fg, bg)` (WCAG 2.x, same module) checks the text colours of `PALETTE` against white and the `surface` grey for at least 4.5:1: `positive` is now `#15803d` (was `#16a34a`, 3.3:1), `caution` `#a16207` (was `#ca8a04`, 2.9:1) and `subtle` equals `muted` (`#6b7280`; `#9ca3af` had 2.5:1), and the footer text and its "logi" link moved from `#9ca3af` to `#6b7280`. Colours hard-coded outside `PALETTE` in other sections (such as `#16a34a` in the portfolio, cost and scorecard sections) are not covered yet.

**Size budget.** Gmail clips messages above ~102 KB and hides the rest, footer and disclaimer included. The HTML is compacted: every run of ASCII whitespace becomes one space (between tags, in text and inside `style` attributes; the mail has no `<pre>`, so it renders identically, and the no-break spaces of the number formatter stay), and the space between two table-structure tags (`</td> <td>`) is dropped. Styles that repeat per symbol live as classes in one `<style>` block (`src/application/report_styles.py`): the predictions table and the compact rows (header row included), the reasoning block and its trend-coloured edge, the top news, the council header and table, the bar charts and the quadrant table, including the direction tone (`up` / `dn` / `nt`, the same palette colours as `delta_color`). What must look the same without the stylesheet stays inline, because the Gmail app with a non-Google account drops `<style>`: the header, the lead, the first-screen numbers, the credibility bar, the signal cards, the section headings and the footer with the disclaimer; the computed bar width stays inline too. Tests keep a typical 8-symbol cycle with a full 7-persona council under 95 KB and a synthetic 45-symbol big-move day, footer and disclaimer included, under 100 KB. When a cycle saves more than 10 predictions, only the top 10 by signal strength (the ranking of "🎯 Najsilniejsze sygnały", symbols without a signal after it in cycle order) and the symbols of the lead keep the full reasoning block with the council table. Every other prediction keeps its row in the predictions table and moves to "📋 Pozostałe predykcje (skrót)" under the reasoning: one compact row with the model signal and the council on one line, the verdict, the consensus, the vote split and the summary (`KUP · zgodność 60% · 🟢 2 KUP · 🔴 0 SPRZEDAJ · 🟡 0 TRZYMAJ — „…”`), with `⚠️ Głosy niezgodne: …` always shown under it. A cycle of up to 10 predictions is rendered in full, and the plain-text email always carries every reasoning in full. The same threshold compacts the 📈 Track Record: above 10 saved predictions it keeps only the key numbers — the equity summary with its cost label ("paper-PnL (koszt 10 bps)"), the "Na tle rynku: agent · koszyk · IVV" line and the directional hit rate with its interval and `n` — and drops the assumptions, both sparkline rows, the calibration table, the interval coverage and the lessons, saying so in one line ("Duży dzień — wersja zwarta …"). A test keeps the 45-symbol day with a full Track Record (cost, benchmark, evidence, Brier, coverage, calibration, lessons) under 100 KB with the disclaimer before byte 100 000: measured 109 627 → 97 859 bytes (disclaimer at 108 964 → 97 196); a typical day keeps the full section (8 symbols: 63 243 bytes). Measured on the synthetic cycles: the 45-symbol day went from ~401 KB to ~274 KB (collapsed council), ~187 KB (compact rows) and ~97 KB (classes and compaction); the typical 8-symbol cycle from ~82 KB to ~51 KB.

All sections are **conditional** — they only render when data exists. The first cold-start cycle produces a concise report; once you have 50+ cycles behind you, accuracy sparklines and history kick in.
